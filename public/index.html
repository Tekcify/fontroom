Questions

Is Fontroom free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the MIT license. No account, nothing to install.

Can I preview fonts on my own website?

Yes. In Fontroom, pick “Your website” under Site, or press L, and paste a link. Your page loads at desktop and phone widths with the fonts you picked. Some single page apps and sites behind bot checks may not load.

Do my font files get uploaded?

No. Your file is read in your browser and stays on your device. It isn't added to share links or saved pairings either.

How do I find a good font pairing?

Set a heading font and a body font, or press Try a pairing for a curated match. Save the ones you like, then flip between them with [ and ] to compare them on the same page.

Does Fontroom support variable fonts?

Yes. The weight menu shows the weights each Google Font really has, including variable ranges. For your own files, Fontroom detects a variable font and writes the @font-face rule to match.

Can I use these fonts in a commercial product?

Google Fonts are open source, so almost always yes. Check the license on the font's Google Fonts page to be sure. For your own files, your font license applies.

Which stacks does the code cover?

Plain HTML and CSS, Tailwind, Next.js with next/font, Flutter with google_fonts, and Expo.

Where are saved pairings kept?

In your browser. Nothing goes to a server, and clearing your browser data removes them.
