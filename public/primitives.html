Fontroom primitives

The tokens and components the clean tool is built from. Every value on this page is the one the app uses.

Colour

--bg --panel --s1…s3 --t1…t4

Surfaces step up in small, even amounts. Text uses four tiers. Colour only marks meaning: the selected state, the main action, success and error. Contrast is measured against the page background.

Slop: a three-stop purple to pink to cyan gradient used on text, buttons, borders and glows, so nothing is more important than anything else.
AI-Powered

Type

Onest · 1 family

One family, a clear scale and restrained weights. Tracking tightens as size grows and never goes past -0.035em. Monospace is only for code and keys.

Spacing

4 · 6 · 8 · 10 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 28 · 40 · 64

Tight inside a group, generous between groups. Areas are separated by space and fills, not borders.

Radius

5 · 7 · 9 · 12 · 16 · 22 · pill

Radius grows with the size of the thing. Pills are only for small chips, never for every button.

Slop: rounded-full on everything, including large buttons, inputs and cards.Get Started 🚀🔍 Search fonts with AI...

Icons

24 grid · 1.75 stroke · currentColor

One drawn set, one stroke weight, coloured by the text around it. Icons sit beside a label unless the control has an accessible name.

Slop: emoji standing in for icons, each with its own colour, style and size.🚀🤖🎯🔒💡
